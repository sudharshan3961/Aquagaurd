01 — UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

When water is
under threat,
life is too.

Water pollution happens when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes, oceans or groundwater, making the water unsafe for people and nature.

It can come from factories, farms, homes and plastic waste. Some pollution is visible, but chemicals and microscopic contaminants can be harder to detect. Understanding where it comes from is the first step toward stopping it.

02 — WHAT POLLUTES OUR WATER?

It starts with us.
It can end with us.

Pollution has many sources. Here are four major contributors that affect water quality around the world.

🏭
01 / INDUSTRY

Industrial waste

Untreated wastewater and chemical discharges can introduce toxic substances and heavy metals into waterways.

🌾
02 / AGRICULTURE

Agricultural runoff

Rain can carry fertilisers, pesticides and animal waste from fields into streams, rivers and lakes.

🧴
03 / PLASTIC

Plastic pollution

Bottles, bags and tiny plastic fragments can harm wildlife and remain in the environment for years.

🏠
04 / HOUSEHOLDS

Domestic sewage

Improperly treated sewage, detergents, oils and household chemicals can contaminate local water sources.

03 — THE RIPPLE EFFECT

One problem.
Many consequences.

Polluted water does not stay in one place. Its effects spread through ecosystems, food chains and communities.

🐟
01 / WILDLIFE

Life below water

Toxic substances, low oxygen and plastic waste can injure or kill aquatic organisms and damage habitats.

💧
02 / HUMAN HEALTH

People at risk

Contaminated drinking and recreational water can spread disease and expose people to harmful chemicals.

🌱
03 / ECOSYSTEMS

Nature out of balance

Excess nutrients can trigger algal blooms, reduce oxygen and disrupt the balance of aquatic ecosystems.

04 — BE PART OF THE SOLUTION

Make waves
for good.

Protecting water is a shared responsibility. Start with practical habits and encourage others to do the same.

  • 01
    Reduce single-use plastic

    Carry reusable bottles and bags; dispose of waste responsibly.

  • 02
    Keep chemicals out of drains

    Never pour oils, paints or medicines into sinks or storm drains.

  • 03
    Use water wisely

    Fix leaks, avoid unnecessary waste and choose water-efficient habits.

  • 04
    Join local clean-ups

    Take part in safe community activities and report illegal dumping.
